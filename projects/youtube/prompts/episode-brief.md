# Episode brief prompt

Create an original illustrated-history documentary episode.

**Channel language:** {{LANGUAGE}}
**Audience:** {{AUDIENCE}}
**Working topic:** {{TOPIC}}
**Story angle:** {{ANGLE}}
**Target duration:** 10–15 minutes

Deliver:
1. A curiosity-driven title and a one-sentence promise.
2. A cold open of 20–30 seconds that starts inside a specific human situation.
3. A structured narrative with estimated timestamps and section goals.
4. A full narration script sized for the target duration (normally around 1,300–1,900 spoken words, adjusted for language and pace).
5. A source note for every material historical claim: source title, author/institution, URL or publication details, and what claim it supports.
6. Explicit labels for uncertain, debated, or reconstructed details. Never present invented dialogue or inner thoughts as documented fact.
7. A visual beat list. Each beat must include timestamp range, what is shown, composition, period-appropriate details, and a standalone image-generation prompt.
8. A thumbnail concept, 3 alternate titles, description, chapters, and relevant search phrases.

Rules:
- Write original material. Do not imitate or paraphrase a reference creator's script.
- Do not invent quotations, statistics, dates, or citations.
- Keep the human perspective central; explain context without turning the episode into a textbook.
- Avoid modern objects, anachronistic clothing, fantasy armor, and inaccurate architecture.
- No copyrighted music or unlicensed third-party images.
- End with a clear, non-clickbait payoff.
